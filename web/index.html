Evidence, not theater
Version one shows actual measurements, source timestamps, missing-data behavior and reproducible historical predictions. No generated "thinking" narrative or untested causal explanation.
2015 and 2017 overlap model training. They are illustrations, not independent tests. May 2024 is from the extra 2021-2024 scoring window. This is a Dst/geomagnetic-storm study, not a solar-flare predictor.
How this updates
Four snapshots a day, planned at 00:17, 06:17, 12:17 and 18:17 UTC. Scheduled jobs can be late or skipped. Observation age is recalculated in your browser even if the next update fails.
A failed ingestion retains the last complete snapshot. Historical replay is fixed, not retrained. No visitor tracking or account is needed.
